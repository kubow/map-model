#python #network #openstreetmap

Python package for downloading, modeling, analyzing, and visualizing OpenStreetMap street networks.

[OSMnx](https://osmnx.readthedocs.io/)

- Builds NetworkX graph models from OSM roads, paths, and other spatial networks.
- Useful for routing, centrality, walkability, street-network metrics, and urban analysis.
- Exports to GeoPackage, GraphML, and other formats.
