#python #analytics #parallel

Parallel and distributed computing library for Python.

[Dask](https://www.dask.org/)

- Useful for chunked geospatial workflows with xarray, rioxarray, GeoPandas, and raster processing.
- Helps process arrays or tabular data that do not fit comfortably in memory.
- Often used with cloud-native raster and data cube workflows.
