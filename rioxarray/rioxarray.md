#python #raster #xarray

Geospatial raster extension for xarray.

[rioxarray](https://corteva.github.io/rioxarray/stable/)

- Adds CRS, transform, reprojection, clipping, and rasterio-backed I/O to xarray objects.
- Useful for raster stacks, NetCDF, GeoTIFF, COG, and time-series gridded data.
- Works well with Dask for larger-than-memory processing.
