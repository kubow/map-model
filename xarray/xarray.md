#python #raster #datacube

Labeled multi-dimensional arrays for Python.

[xarray](https://docs.xarray.dev/)

- Useful for NetCDF, Zarr, climate data, raster stacks, and data cubes.
- Handles dimensions, coordinates, metadata, and grouped operations more naturally than raw NumPy arrays.
- Often paired with rioxarray, Dask, Zarr, and STAC workflows.
