#tileset #tool #vector-tiles

Command line tool for building vector tiles from GeoJSON, Geobuf, and CSV data.

[mapbox/tippecanoe](https://github.com/mapbox/tippecanoe)

- Produces MBTiles or tile directories.
- Useful when large vector datasets need simplification, zoom-based filtering, or tiling before web display.
- Often paired with MapLibre, PMTiles, TileServer GL, or custom tile services.
