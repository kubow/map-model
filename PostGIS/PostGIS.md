#database #server #analytics

Spatial database extender for PostgreSQL.

[PostGIS](https://postgis.net/)

- Adds geometry/geography types and spatial indexes to PostgreSQL.
- Core choice for multi-user vector storage, spatial SQL, topology, routing extensions, and publishing data to services.
- Works well with QGIS, GeoServer, MapServer, [pg_featureserv](pg_featureserv.md), [pg_tileserv](pg_tileserv.md), and GDAL/OGR.
