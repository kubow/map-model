#database #analytics #python #tool

Embedded analytical database with a spatial extension.

[DuckDB Spatial Extension](https://duckdb.org/docs/stable/core_extensions/spatial/overview.html)

- Useful for local spatial SQL without running a server.
- Reads many geospatial formats through GDAL/OGR.
- Good fit for ad hoc analysis, notebooks, ETL, GeoParquet, Parquet, and quick joins before publishing data elsewhere.
