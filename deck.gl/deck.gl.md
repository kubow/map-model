#javascript #visualization #webgl

WebGL framework for large-scale geospatial visualization.

[deck.gl](https://deck.gl/)

- Handles high-volume point, line, polygon, grid, heatmap, tile, and 3D layers.
- Usually used with MapLibre, Mapbox GL, Google Maps, or as a standalone canvas.
- Good fit for analytical frontends where Leaflet/OpenLayers become too slow.
