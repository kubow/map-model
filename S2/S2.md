#indexing #analytics #grid

Hierarchical spherical geometry and cell indexing library.

[S2 Geometry](https://s2geometry.io/)

- Represents the sphere using a hierarchy of cells.
- Useful for indexing, coverings, proximity work, and robust spherical geometry.
- Often compared with H3, geohash, and quadkeys.
