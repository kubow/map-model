#indexing #analytics #grid

Hierarchical hexagonal geospatial indexing system.

[H3](https://h3geo.org/)

- Converts coordinates and shapes into global hexagonal cell indexes.
- Useful for aggregation, spatial joins, mobility analysis, coverage, and multi-resolution summaries.
- Bindings exist for many languages and databases.
