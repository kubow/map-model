SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog.

[STAC](https://stacspec.org/)

- Common metadata model for discovering and querying geospatial assets.
- Originally focused on satellite imagery, now also used for DEMs, SAR, lidar, vectors, labels, videos, and derived products.
- Often paired with COG, Zarr, GeoParquet, TiTiler, pystac, and cloud object storage.
