Geospatial metadata and geometry encoding for Apache Parquet.

[GeoParquet](https://geoparquet.org/)

- Columnar format for geospatial vector data lakes and analytics.
- Works well with DuckDB, GeoPandas, pyarrow, cloud storage, and large tabular datasets.
- Better suited than GeoJSON for large analytical datasets.
- A flat file, but columnar and metadata-rich rather than a row-oriented text file.
